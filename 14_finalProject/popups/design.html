REFLECTION
Looking back, I’m really happy with how my final project turned out. I was able to get it done and then later tweak several variables that significantly improved its performance. That said, I’m not completely satisfied with the project itself. Much of the work ended up being digital, and I wish I had more hands-on involvement. Also, in its current state, the project doesn’t have a clear real-world application. It’s not a river cleaner made from recycled materials or a portable incubator designed for medical use (other projects from my class-check them out!), it’s simply something I found interesting rather than something genuinely novel. That being said, I’m committed to continuing this work. With further improvements, especially optimizing it to scan larger spaces, I hope to remedy some of the limitations I mentioned earlier and make a useful tool. This would include, additionally, exploring creating my own LiDAR sensor, though more to come on that later. Stay tuned!
