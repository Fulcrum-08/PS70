LiDAR Scanner Design Process

Workflow Overview

  • Research & Requirements Gathering
  • Component Selection & Procurement
  • Hardware Assembly & Integration
  • Firmware Development & Calibration
  • Data Acquisition & Processing
  • Testing & Iteration
  • Final Deployment & Documentation

Milestones

  • Week 1-2: Define project goals and specifications, select sensor and microcontroller.
  • Week 3-4: Build prototype hardware; initial laser and sensor integration.
  • Week 5-6: Develop firmware for sensor communication and stepper motor control.
  • Week 7-8: Implement scanning algorithm and data processing pipeline.
  • Week 9-10: Test accuracy, optimize scanning speed and resolution.
  • Week 11: Finalize design and prepare documentation for users and developers.

Key Decisions

  • Choosing the laser distance sensor: VL53L0X for its time-of-flight accuracy.
  • Microcontroller selection: ESP32 for wireless capability and sufficient processing power.
  • Mechanical design: Use stepper motors for precise angular control of scanning.
  • Data handling: Perform basic processing on microcontroller, offload visualization to PC.
  • Power considerations: Optimize for low power consumption for potential battery operation.
